# Rules for the manifest-based comparison set

**Fixed 2026-10-31, before any comparison was recomputed.** These rules replace the
regular-expression screens in `analyze_pds.py`, `screen_dryad.py` and
`variable_classes.py`, which an independent review (`Research_Review/PROJECT_REVIEW.md`)
showed had admitted non-randomized groupings, baseline variables, an undisclosed
effect-size filter, and file-order caps. Every trial now has a manifest in
`manifest/trials/<trial>.yaml`, generated by `manifest/write_manifests.py`, which names the
allocation variable, the randomization unit, every data table, and the role and timing of
every variable that enters, with a one-line evidence note. `code/build_comparisons.py`
reads only the manifests. It applies no name-based guessing.

## 1. Trials

1. A trial enters if it randomized individual participants to two or more arms and the
   deposited data identify the randomized assignment. The assignment variable is the one
   the data dictionary or protocol calls the randomized or planned treatment (`TRT`,
   `TRT01P`, `randf`, `ARM`, `group_randomized`, `randassign`), never the treatment
   received (`ATRT`, `TRT01A`, `SAFGROUP`).
2. Screen failures, cancelled and never-treated participants are excluded only where the
   deposit flags them as not randomized (`RANDFL = N`, `SCREEN FAILURE`). Participants
   randomized but later excluded from the trial's own analysis are retained: the unit is
   the randomized population.
3. Cluster-randomized trials are excluded from the primary set and listed in the ledger.
   The participant-level tests being compared are not valid for them as deposited.
4. Factorial trials contribute one comparison per factor margin per endpoint, which is how
   the trials analysed themselves. Multi-arm trials contribute every pairwise contrast
   among arms with at least 20 observed participants.

## 2. Variables

5. A variable enters as an **outcome** when the data dictionary, CRF or codebook shows it
   was measured after the first dose of allocated treatment and records a response of the
   participant: laboratory values, vital signs, tumour measurements, patient-reported
   scores, physiological tests, anthropometry, and clinical measurements such as operative
   time or length of stay. Roles recorded: `primary`, `secondary`, `safety_lab`, `vital`,
   `tumour`, `pro`, `clinical`.
6. A variable enters as a **negative control** when it was measured before randomization:
   screening or baseline visits, variables prefixed `B_`, baseline-labelled columns, age,
   and history variables.
7. Measurements taken after randomization but before the first dose (`Cycle 1 Day 1`
   pre-dose, `Week 0`, `Pre-Treatment`) are classed `pre_dose` and excluded from both
   sets, because their timing relative to exposure cannot be established from the data.
8. Excluded as a class, with the reason recorded: identifiers and codes; dates and study
   days; delivered dose, number of doses, cycles, infusions, exposure duration and
   compliance; censored event times and any duration subject to censoring (survival,
   progression, time to full oral feeds, length of stay where a censoring flag exists);
   change-from-baseline and percent-change derivations, best-response and minimum-over-
   follow-up derivations; duplicate recordings of the same quantity; per-item ordinal
   responses; and process measures of the intervention (messages sent, interventions
   completed, protocol deviations). COST's operative time, hospital stay and narcotic days
   carry no censoring and are retained.
9. A variable must have at least 10 distinct values to count as continuous. This is a
   measurement property, not an outcome property.

## 3. Visits

10. For a repeated measure, one comparison is taken per endpoint per arm contrast at the
    **first scheduled post-dose visit at which both arms have at least 20 observed
    values**. This is the visit with the fullest randomized population and the least
    selection by discontinuation. The last scheduled visit meeting the same condition is
    recorded as a sensitivity comparison and reported separately.
11. The negative control for a repeated measure is the screening or baseline visit of the
    same endpoint.
12. Unscheduled, retest, discontinuation, end-of-treatment, safety-follow-up and
    post-progression visits are excluded, because attendance at them is determined by
    post-randomization events.

## 4. Samples and tests

13. At least 20 observed participants per arm, applied per comparison.
14. No filter references effect size, significance, AUC, or distribution shape.
15. For every comparison the Welch t-test and the two-sided Mann-Whitney test are computed
    under seven specifications on the recorded scale, oriented as deposited:
    - strictly increasing (Proposition 1 applies exactly): recorded; logarithm after
      shifting the pooled sample to have minimum equal to one percent of its range when
      the minimum is at or below zero; van der Waerden normal scores of the pooled sample;
    - coarsening (ties created, Proposition 1 does not apply): winsorizing at the pooled
      1st/99th and 5th/95th percentiles;
    - deleting: trimming at the same percentiles.
    All seven p-values are stored. Spans and verdict changes are computed downstream for
    each menu separately. Before any specification is applied, the pooled values of a
    comparison are rounded to nine significant figures relative to their largest
    magnitude. Deposited files carry floating-point noise (−0.331 beside
    −0.33099999999999996) that a rank treats as two values and a shifted logarithm
    collapses into one; the rounding exceeds any clinical measurement's precision and
    makes the rank-based quantities exactly invariant, as Proposition 1 states.
16. For every comparison the empirical ROC curve of the second arm against the first is
    computed on the recorded scale, with its area (the probabilistic index), the
    Cramér-von Mises and Kolmogorov-Smirnov departures from the diagonal, and a
    permutation p-value for the Cramér-von Mises statistic from 999 label permutations
    with a fixed seed. These are invariant under the strictly increasing menu by
    construction and are computed once.

## 5. Ledger

17. Every candidate variable in every manifest table is written to
    `results/exclusion_ledger.csv` with its disposition: included as outcome, included
    as negative control, or excluded with the rule number above. Every deposit screened
    but not manifested is listed with the reason.
